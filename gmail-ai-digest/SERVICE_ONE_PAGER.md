# AI Email Intelligence Pilot

## Turn a busy shared inbox into an actionable daily digest

InboxPilot organizes incoming email into clear business categories, summarizes each message, highlights urgency and exports a structured report—helping teams spend less time scanning and more time acting.

### Ideal for

Recruitment agencies, consultancies, finance teams, support desks and operations teams managing repetitive email.

### Pilot deliverables

- Connection to one Gmail inbox using read-only OAuth access
- Up to five customized business categories
- AI-generated summaries, priority and sentiment labels
- Search, category filters and daily CSV export
- Responsive dashboard and basic deployment
- One training session and seven days of post-delivery support

### Delivery plan

| Stage | Timeline |
|---|---|
| Workflow discovery and category design | Day 1 |
| Gmail connection and classification setup | Days 2–3 |
| Dashboard customization and testing | Days 4–5 |
| Deployment, training and handover | Days 6–7 |

Monthly monitoring, prompt improvement and minor category changes from **₹3,000 per month**.

A consistent, searchable email briefing that helps staff identify important messages faster and creates structured information for reporting and follow-up.
