# 75-second demo script

## 0–10 seconds — The problem

**Screen:** Open a crowded email inbox screenshot, then switch to InboxPilot.

**Voiceover:** “Teams lose time scanning routine emails just to find the few messages that actually need action. InboxPilot turns that inbox into a prioritized daily briefing.”

## 10–28 seconds — Instant overview

**Screen:** Point to Messages Analyzed, Needs Attention and Time Saved.

**Voiceover:** “The dashboard analyzes today’s messages and immediately shows volume, urgent items and estimated review time saved.”

## 28–48 seconds — AI intelligence

**Screen:** Scroll through cards and highlight summary, category, priority and sentiment.

**Voiceover:** “Every email receives a concise summary, business category, priority and sentiment signal, so users understand the message without opening every thread.”

## 48–62 seconds — Find and act

**Screen:** Select Important, search for ‘proposal’, then clear the search.

**Voiceover:** “Teams can isolate important messages or search the AI summaries to find a decision, deadline or customer request in seconds.”

## 62–75 seconds — Export and close

**Screen:** Click Export CSV and briefly show the downloaded report.

**Voiceover:** “The structured digest exports to CSV for reporting and follow-up. InboxPilot can be adapted to recruitment, finance, support or operations workflows.”

**Recording note:** Use 1080p, hide browser bookmarks and notifications, keep zoom at 100%, and record the voiceover after capturing the clean screen sequence.
